📐 CSAT & Aptitude Math Speed Shortcuts ▼

Average Speed: When distance is same: \( \text{Avg Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x+y} \).

Speed Conversion: \( \text{km/h} \times \frac{5}{18} = \text{m/s} \), \( \text{m/s} \times \frac{18}{5} = \text{km/h} \).

Fraction to %: \( \frac{1}{6} = 16.66\% \), \( \frac{1}{7} = 14.28\% \), \( \frac{1}{8} = 12.5\% \), \( \frac{1}{9} = 11.11\% \), \( \frac{1}{12} = 8.33\% \).

Train & Platform: Time to cross platform \( = \frac{\text{Length of Train} + \text{Length of Platform}}{\text{Speed of Train}} \).
