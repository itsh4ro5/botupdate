🏛️ Indian Constitution: Core Articles & Writs ▼

Art. 14–18: Right to Equality (Art 14 Equality before Law, Art 17 Abolition of Untouchability).

Art. 19–22: Right to Freedom (Art 19 Six Freedoms, Art 21 Right to Life & Personal Liberty).

Art. 32: Right to Constitutional Remedies (5 Writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo-Warranto).

Art. 36–51: Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) — Non-justiciable in courts.

Art. 352, 356, 360: Emergency Provisions (National, President's Rule, Financial).

📐 CSAT & Aptitude Math Speed Shortcuts ▼

Average Speed: When distance is equal: \( \text{Avg Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x+y} \).

Speed Conversion: \( \text{km/h} \times \frac{5}{18} = \text{m/s} \), \( \text{m/s} \times \frac{18}{5} = \text{km/h} \).

Fraction to %: \( \frac{1}{6} = 16.66\% \), \( \frac{1}{7} = 14.28\% \), \( \frac{1}{8} = 12.5\% \), \( \frac{1}{9} = 11.11\% \), \( \frac{1}{12} = 8.33\% \).

Train & Platform: Time to cross platform \( = \frac{\text{Length of Train} + \text{Length of Platform}}{\text{Speed of Train}} \).

🌍 Indian Rivers & Tributaries Blueprint ▼

Ganga Left Bank: Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Mahananda.

Ganga Right Bank: Yamuna (Chambal, Betwa, Ken), Son, Punpun.

West-Flowing Rivers: Narmada (Amarkantak), Tapi, Sabarmati, Mahi, Periyar.

✍️ Mains Answer Writing Structure (3-Tier Model) ▼

1. Introduction (15%): Define the keyword, cite standard committee/data (e.g. NITI Aayog, NCRB, Economic Survey) or constitutional article.

2. Body (70%): Break into 3-4 subheadings using PESTLE framework (Political, Economic, Social, Tech, Legal, Environmental). Use bullet points and box diagrams.

3. Conclusion (15%): Forward-looking "Way Forward", SDG goal linkage, or quote from leaders/Constitution preamble.
